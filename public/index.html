Setup required

1 Verify the applicant's identity Protean Aadhaar eKYC
bills ekyc_otp_sent + ekyc_verifications

An OTP goes to the mobile registered against the applicant's Aadhaar. Entering it returns their name, date of birth and address straight from the source, so the branch never keys those in by hand.

2 Confirm PAN and its Aadhaar link Protean PAN Verification
bills pan_verifications + pan_link_checks

Two separate questions. Is the PAN real and active, and is it seeded against this Aadhaar? An account cannot open on a surrendered PAN, and an unlinked one has to be resolved before the first tax report.

3 Collect supporting documents Protean DigiLocker
bills documents_fetched

Listing what the applicant holds is free. Each document you actually pull is billed and separately consented — agreeing to share a PAN card is not agreeing to share a driving licence.

4 Sign the account opening form Protean Aadhaar eSign
bills esign_requests

Only the document's SHA-256 leaves the bank. Protean signs the hash, never sees the form, and returns a signature ICICI stores against the application.
